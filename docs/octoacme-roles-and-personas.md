# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing

### Role Summary
QA validates quality and acceptance criteria and ensures releases meet reliability standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans for features and releases
- Execute manual and automated tests
- Verify acceptance criteria and regression coverage
- Coordinate with Developers and Release Manager on verification steps

---

## UX Designer (new)

### Role Summary
UX Designers ensure product decisions reflect user needs and usability best practices across the lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specs
- Collaborate with Product Managers to define user-facing acceptance criteria
- Work with Developers to ensure designs are implemented as intended
- Advocate for accessibility and inclusive design

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Product Manager (requirements and acceptance criteria)
- Partners with Developers during implementation and iteration
- Shares research and prototypes with Stakeholders for alignment

---

## Scrum Master (new)

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate the team's Agile process and help the delivery team continuously improve.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate agile ceremonies (standups, sprint planning, retrospectives)
- Help remove team impediments and surface blockers
- Coach the team on agreed practices and Definition of Done
- Support cross-team coordination for dependencies

### Interaction with existing roles
- Coordinates with Project Manager to surface schedule risks and dependencies
- Works with Developers and QA to improve flow and throughput
- Partners with Product Manager on backlog readiness and refinement

---

## Release Manager (new)

### Role Summary
Release Managers plan, coordinate, and validate releases to reduce deployment risk and ensure post-deploy verification.

### Responsibilities
- Own release readiness checklist and schedule
- Coordinate staging and production deployments with Engineering, QA, and Ops
- Ensure rollback/mitigation plans and runbooks are available
- Run post-deploy verifications and capture release notes

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Project Manager to schedule deployment windows
- Coordinates with QA for smoke and acceptance testing
- Aligns with Product Manager and Stakeholders on release communications

---

## Business Analyst (new)

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business stakeholders and delivery teams by clarifying requirements and acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document requirements from stakeholders
- Translate business needs into clear backlog items and acceptance criteria
- Support Product Manager and Developers in clarifying ambiguous requirements
- Validate delivered functionality against business needs

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Manager to prioritize and scope features
- Collaborates with Developers and QA to ensure shared understanding
- Engages Stakeholders to validate assumptions

---

## Security Lead (new)

### Role Summary
Security Leads ensure security is considered throughout design, development, and release processes.

### Responsibilities
- Provide threat modeling and security requirements
- Review designs and code for security risks
- Coordinate security scans and remediation priorities
- Advise on incident response and disclosure when required

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to remediate findings
- Coordinates with Release Manager on deployment gating if security risks are unresolved
- Informs Project Manager of security-related schedule impacts

---

## Data Analyst (new)

### Role Summary
Data Analysts help define and measure success metrics, ensuring instrumentation and metric collection are in place.

### Responsibilities
- Define measurement plans and instrumentation needs
- Provide dashboards and reports for feature impact
- Analyze usage and performance data to inform decisions

### Interaction with existing roles
- Collaborates with Product Manager on success metrics
- Works with Developers to ensure telemetry is implemented
- Advises Project Manager and Stakeholders on data-driven priorities

---

## Stakeholder Liaison (new)

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder Liaison maintains clear external communication and coordinates stakeholder involvement and expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain stakeholder contact list and communication cadence
- Draft and send stakeholder updates and decisions needed
- Collect stakeholder feedback and route to Product/Project teams

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Project Manager to align communications
- Escalates issues to Product Manager or Sponsor when needed

---
